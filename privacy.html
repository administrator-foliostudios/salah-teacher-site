Privacy policy

Salah Teacher, by Folio App Studios · Effective 16 November 2026

SupportPrivacy policy
In shortSalah Teacher does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPad.

What stays on your iPad

The app saves your settings, how long you take on each page in Practise, and the prayers you tick in the Tracker. These are stored only on your iPad and are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

If you allow it, Salah Teacher uses your iPad's location to work out prayer times and the direction of the Qibla. This calculation happens on your iPad. To show the name of your town or city, the app asks iOS to look it up, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your location.

You can refuse or turn off location at any time in the iPad's Settings and choose your city in the app instead.

Compass

The Qibla compass reads the iPad's heading while the Times & Qibla screen is open. The heading is used on your iPad only and is not stored.

Notifications

If you turn on the check-in reminder, the app schedules a notification on your iPad. Notifications are created on the device; nothing is sent from a server.

Children

Salah Teacher is made for learners of all ages, including children. Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code, and we do not sell or share any data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
